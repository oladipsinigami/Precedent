# Research Workbench — Design

## Core Concept
A focused research surface for tokenized US stocks.  
The AI gathers and synthesizes evidence.  
The human decides.

Tone: calm, precise, institutional.  
Never signal-like. Always research-like.

---

## Mode Switching (woven in)

At the start of every session the workbench asks:

**What kind of trader are you operating as right now?**

- Day  
- Swing  
- Event / Macro  
- Position  

This choice is not a setting buried in a menu.  
It lives at the top of the research flow and reshapes:

- Depth of analysis  
- Time horizon language  
- What “considerations” emphasize  
- How historical analogs are framed  

The selected mode remains visible and can be changed at any time without losing the current research thread.

---

## Flexibility

The interface must feel adaptable, not rigid.

- Research depth expands or contracts based on mode  
- Columns / panels can be collapsed or expanded  
- Synthesis can be viewed as a continuous narrative or as discrete evidence blocks  
- Historical analogs can be shown as a compact strip or an expanded comparison  
- The same research question can be re-run under a different mode without starting over  

The design prioritizes fluid control over fixed layouts.

---

## Layout Principles

- Single primary research column with optional secondary panels  
- Evidence → Tension → Historical Analogs → Considerations remains the structural spine  
- Mode selector is always present but never dominant  
- Minimal chrome. Maximum clarity of the research output  
- Dark base with high-contrast typography for long reading sessions  

---

## Visual Language

- Dark background  
- Clean sans for body and UI  
- Restrained display treatment for section labels  
- Soft borders and subtle elevation only where hierarchy is needed  
- No decorative cards competing with the research content  

---

## Interaction Notes

- Mode change feels instant and intentional  
- Switching modes re-weights the same underlying data rather than reloading a new page  
- The trader can always see which mode is active  
- Empty or loading states stay quiet and non-blocking
